# LNG-Bunkering-Station-BOM-Cost-Analysis
## Overview

LNG-Bunkering-Station-BOM-Cost-Analysis is a comprehensive engineering and cost estimation project focused on developing a detailed Bill of Materials (BOM) and cost analysis framework for Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) bunkering stations. The project provides a structured methodology for identifying infrastructure components, estimating capital costs, and evaluating the economic feasibility of LNG bunkering facilities.

## Objectives

- Develop a detailed LNG bunkering station BOM.
- Categorize mechanical, electrical, instrumentation, and civil components.
- Estimate capital expenditure (CAPEX).
- Analyze cost distribution across major systems.
- Identify key cost drivers and optimization opportunities.
- Support feasibility studies and investment planning.
